# OMSCS Hub

OMSCS Hub helps students explore courses, compare experiences, and plan progress through the Online Master of Science in Computer Science program.

## Language

**Catalog**:
The curated set of courses and course facts presented consistently across course browsing and planning.

**Course**:
A distinct OMSCS course that a student can explore, review, or place in a study plan.

**Specialization**:
A published path through the degree with requirements against which a study plan can show progress. Progress in OMSCS Hub is guidance, not a determination of degree completion.
_Avoid_: Track

**Study Plan**:
A student's completed courses and intended courses and terms, including intended courses not yet assigned to a term.
_Avoid_: Planner state

**Completed Course**:
A course a student records as finished. It may have multiple attempts, but is distinct from a course the student only intends to take.

**Course Attempt**:
One occasion on which a student took a course, identified by its term and outcome. Repeating a course creates another attempt of the same course.

**Estimated GPA**:
A GPA calculated from the course attempts a student entered in OMSCS Hub. It is not the student's official Georgia Tech GPA.

**Hub Review**:
A course review written in OMSCS Hub by a verified Georgia Tech account. Its author can edit or delete it.
_Avoid_: App review

**Imported Review**:
A course review brought into OMSCS Hub from OMSCentral. It is read-only within OMSCS Hub.
_Avoid_: Seeded review
